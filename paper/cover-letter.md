# Cover letter

Target journal: **Nonlinearity** (IOP Publishing / London Mathematical Society).

Chosen because the family itself was introduced there: Okamoto, Sakajo and
Wunsch, *On a generalization of the Constantin, Lax and Majda equation*,
Nonlinearity **21** (2008) 2447, which is `osw2008` in the bibliography. The
editorial board has handled this exact problem. Publication on the subscription
route is free of charge; the article publication charge applies only if gold
open access is chosen after acceptance.

Submission notes, not part of the letter:

- Upload `paper/arxiv/degregorio-anon.pdf`, built by
  `python arxiv_package.py --anon`. IOP wants a single PDF at initial
  submission and asks for source only on acceptance, which is when
  `degregorio-anon.tar.gz` becomes the relevant file. Nonlinearity defaults to
  double anonymous review, and the anonymised build is the one that belongs in
  the portal.
- The letter goes to the editor rather than the referees, so it carries the
  author's name even under double anonymous review.
- The abstract is 296 words against their limit of 300.

To the Editors of *Nonlinearity*

Re: "The frozen blowup profile of the Okamoto, Sakajo and Wunsch family: a
control variate for its spectral exponent"

Dear Editors,

I am submitting the above manuscript for consideration as a research paper.

The Okamoto, Sakajo and Wunsch family was introduced in this journal
(Nonlinearity 21 (2008) 2447), and the paper concerns the frozen blowup profile
it admits on the circle above a critical advection. That profile's Fourier
spectrum decays algebraically with an exponent beta which has previously been
obtained by least squares fits to the spectrum. I show that fitting is accurate
only to about a percent and is biased, and give a different determination: the
exponent follows from a single local quantity, and the difficulty of reading
that quantity off a discrete profile is removed by using a second constant,
known exactly, as a control variate. This yields five digits, excludes the value
beta = 3 at a = 0.8, and corrects the published exponent at a = 0.71 by 0.33
percent.

I should draw your attention to two things. First, the setting is not mine: the
frozen profile was found numerically by Lushnikov, Silantyev and Siegel, and its
existence, stability and local exponent near a = 1 were proved by Chen. The
introduction and a Limitations section state in detail what is theirs and what
is mine, and the contribution is deliberately narrow. Second, the paper corrects
a value published by Lushnikov, Silantyev and Siegel. I have written to them
describing the discrepancy and offering the manuscript, before submitting here.

I request double-anonymous review. The manuscript is not under consideration
elsewhere, has not been published previously, and there are no competing
interests. I am an independent researcher with no institutional affiliation and
no funding to declare.

Yours faithfully,

Munawar Kazmi
